# Changelog

What changed for users, newest first. Add a line under "Unreleased" in every pull request.

## Unreleased

## 3.0.0 · 2026-11-18

- Tasks are saved on a server, so they're the same in every browser and on every device.
- Changes show at once and are confirmed in the background.
- The filter is part of the address (`/?filter=active`): reload, share and Back all keep it.
- A blank task gets a clear message from the server.
- Your light or dark choice is remembered by the server, so the page never flashes.
- Rebuilt on Next.js; tasks saved in the browser by version 2 are not carried over.

## 2.0.0 · 2026-11-18

- Tasks have a checkbox, and the footer shows how many are left.
- Filter the list: All, Active, Done.
- "Clear completed" shows only when there is something to clear.
- Long or blank tasks are tidied: spaces trimmed, 200 characters at most.
- The project is reorganised for teamwork and AI agents (see README). Tasks saved by v1 still load.

## 1.0.0

- Add, complete and delete tasks; saved in the browser.
- Clear completed tasks.
- Dark mode that remembers your choice.
- The heading reads "The Best App".
